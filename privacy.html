Privacy Policy

Rowan, by Animum Rege LLC. Last updated 2026-09-29.

What we collect

Nothing. Rowan has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and does not send your data to us or to anyone else. We do not receive your vault, your contacts, your usage, or any personal information.

Where your data lives

Everything you add to Rowan (vault items, your circle and your plan) is stored only on your phone, inside the app.

Starting with version 1.3.0, Rowan encrypts your vault on your phone as soon as you set up your Recovery Kit: 12 words that Rowan shows you once, to write on paper. Rowan never stores those words and never sends them anywhere, and we cannot recover them for you. Your phone’s backups (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone, or Google backup on Android) include only the encrypted vault. Restoring it on a phone needs your Recovery Kit words.

Rowan also keeps a small encrypted recovery copy with your phone’s own cloud backup service (iCloud on iPhone, Google’s Block Store on Android), so a new phone can find your vault. The copy can only be opened with your Recovery Kit words. Neither we nor Apple nor Google can read it.

Before version 1.3.0, and on 1.3.0 until you set up your Recovery Kit, Rowan’s data is protected by your phone’s lock screen and, like other app data, is included in your phone’s backups. Anyone who can restore those backups could restore Rowan’s data with them. That is also true of any backup made before you updated to 1.3.0, until the backup is replaced or deleted.

Camera and microphone

Starting with version 1.3.0, you can add photos, videos and voice notes to your vault. Rowan uses the camera and the microphone only when you choose to take a photo or video or record a voice note. What you record goes straight into your vault, encrypted on your phone, and Rowan never sends it anywhere. On Android, photos, videos and voice notes are not included in your phone’s backups and stay on the phone where you added them. On iPhone, they are part of the encrypted vault in your backup.

Your circle

In this version, your circle is planned and previewed on your own phone. The names you add are not sent to those people or to us, and nothing is delivered to anyone.

Releasing your vault

In this version, a release is a private preview on your phone that shows what each person would receive. Delivery to your circle — requiring the number of people you chose to agree, with a challenge window during which you can say “I’m fine” — is coming in a future update. We will update this policy before that update ships.

Payments

Any purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes Rowan’s data from your phone. Starting with version 1.1, you can also delete everything from Settings in the app. Starting with version 1.3.0, that also deletes this vault’s encrypted recovery copy from your phone’s cloud backup service. If Rowan can’t reach that service at the time, it tells you and lets you try again. Copies in backups made earlier remain until those backups are replaced or deleted.
