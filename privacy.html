Privacy Policy

Rowan, by Animum Rege LLC. Last updated 2026-09-24.

What we collect

Nothing. Rowan has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and does not send your data to us or to anyone else. We do not receive your vault, your contacts, your usage, or any personal information.

Where your data lives

Everything you add to Rowan — vault items, your circle, and your plan — is stored only on your phone, inside the app, and is protected by your phone’s lock screen. Like other app data, it is included in your phone’s backups (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone, or Google backup on Android). Anyone who can restore those backups could restore Rowan’s data with them.

Your circle

In this version, your circle is planned and previewed on your own phone. The names you add are not sent to those people or to us, and nothing is delivered to anyone.

Releasing your vault

In this version, a release is a private preview on your phone that shows what each person would receive. Delivery to your circle — requiring the number of people you chose to agree, with a challenge window during which you can say “I’m fine” — is coming in a future update. We will update this policy before that update ships.

Payments

Any purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes Rowan’s data from your phone. Starting with version 1.1, you can also delete everything from Settings in the app. Copies in backups made earlier remain until those backups are replaced or deleted.
