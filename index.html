Rowan

A quiet plan for the people you trust.

Rowan helps you gather what matters — account details, documents, letters, and final wishes — choose a small circle of people you trust, and decide exactly who would receive what. Then preview a release to see what each person would get.

This version is a preview. Your vault, your circle, and your plan stay on your phone, and nothing is sent to the people you add yet. Delivery to your circle — with a group vote and a chance for you to say “I’m fine” — is coming in a future update.

How it works

  • Stage your vault. Documents, account details, final wishes.
  • Leave Forget-Me-Nots. Letters for the people you love.
  • Build your circle. Add 3–5 people you trust, each checked with a safety number.
  • Choose the rules. How many must agree, and who receives what.
  • Preview a release. See exactly what each person would receive.

Price

Starting with version 1.1, everything in Rowan is free during the preview — no membership needed. No account, no ads, no tracking.
