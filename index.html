Rowan

A quiet plan for the people you trust.

Rowan lets you pre-stage an encrypted vault — account details, letters, voice notes, and final wishes — and choose a small circle of people you trust. If something happens to you, your circle can open exactly what you left them, but only when the number of people you chose agree, and only after a challenge window during which you can say “I’m fine.”

Built so no one but your circle can ever open it — not us, and not Apple. Everything is encrypted on your device before it’s stored, and Rowan runs no servers of its own.

How it works

  • Stage your vault. Add what matters. It’s encrypted on your device first.
  • Build your circle. Add 3–5 people you trust and verify each in person.
  • Choose the rules. How many must agree, and who receives what.
  • Make it real. Arm your circle. If it’s ever needed, they vote, you get a loud chance to stop it, and only then is your vault opened — to the right people.

Membership

Rowan is free to build and preview. A membership keeps your circle live and lets you manage it — 30 days free, then $19.99/year (or $2.99/month). A circle you’ve already armed keeps protecting your family even if a membership lapses; a lapse only pauses your ability to make changes.
